ChatGPT for Productivity — Work Smarter, Not Harder

The highest-ROI productivity use for ChatGPT isn't generating content — it's thinking. Using AI as a thinking partner for decisions, planning, and prioritization saves hours every week.

Best productivity tasks for ChatGPT

  • Summarize anything: paste a 20-page report, get 5 bullet points in 10 seconds
  • Prioritize tasks: give it your task list, ask for Eisenhower Matrix sorting
  • Break down goals: "I want to launch a product in 3 months — give me a week-by-week plan"
  • Draft templates: repetitive emails, status updates, meeting agendas
  • Think through decisions: "List pros, cons, risks and second-order effects of [decision]"

The 2-minute rule for AI productivity

If a task takes under 2 minutes, do it yourself. If it takes 2–20 minutes and involves writing, drafting, or researching — try AI first. If it takes over 20 minutes and requires judgment or relationships — use AI for the prep work (summarize the brief, draft the outline, generate options) and do the high-value part yourself.

Summarizing long documents — the 2-pass method

  1. Split the document into sections (chapters, pages, topics)
  2. Paste each section and ask for a 3-bullet summary
  3. Paste all the section summaries together and ask for a 1-page executive summary
  4. Ask: "What are the 3 most important decisions or action items?"
Is AI better for planning or for doing?

AI is better for planning, drafting, and thinking — not for doing. It generates options, frameworks, and first drafts faster than any human. The doing — making the decision, building the relationship, shipping the product — remains human. Use AI to reduce the time you spend in 'pre-work' mode so you can spend more time in 'execution' mode.
