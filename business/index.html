ChatGPT for Business — Strategy, Operations & Communication

Business prompts work best with Claude or ChatGPT-4. For long documents like business plans and SOPs, Claude handles structure better. For shorter, persuasive content like stakeholder updates and executive summaries, both perform equally well.

Getting useful business analysis from ChatGPT

  • Provide industry context: "We are a B2B SaaS company targeting mid-sized logistics firms in India"
  • Give specific numbers when available: revenue, growth rate, headcount, market size
  • Name competitors explicitly — ChatGPT gives sharper analysis with real names
  • Ask for the output in a specific format: table, numbered list, section headers
  • For SWOT: ask ChatGPT to prioritize the top 3 items in each quadrant, not list 10

Writing SOPs with AI — the right workflow

1. Write the current process in 10 bullet points (messy is fine). 2. Ask ChatGPT to clean it into a numbered SOP with decision branches ("If X, go to Step 3"). 3. Ask it to identify gaps: "What steps might I have missed?" 4. Add role assignments and timeframes yourself — ChatGPT doesn't know your team. 5. Have the actual process owner review before publishing.

Meeting agendas — before vs after AI

A typical "before AI" meeting agenda: a 3-bullet list sent 5 minutes before the call. An AI-assisted agenda: goal, pre-read materials, timed sections, owner for each item, expected output, parking lot section. Use the Meeting Agenda prompt and tell it the meeting's single most important decision — that becomes the anchor for everything else.

Can ChatGPT help with investor pitches?

Yes — for structuring the narrative and drafting slide content. Describe your business, traction, market size, team, and ask ChatGPT to draft the problem-solution-traction-ask arc. It won't know your actual metrics, so you'll need to add those. Where AI is most useful for pitches: drafting the one-liner, the "why now" slide, and the competitive landscape framing. The story and conviction must come from you.
