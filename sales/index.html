ChatGPT for Sales — Cold Email, Outreach & Proposals

Sales is the most measurable use case for AI writing. A better cold email subject line can double open rates. A personalised first sentence can triple reply rates. These prompts give you proven frameworks — fill in your details and edit to match your voice.

The anatomy of a high-converting cold email

  • Subject line: 4–7 words, specific, avoid spam triggers (FREE, URGENT, !)
  • Opening line: personalised to the prospect — something specific about them
  • Pain point: one sentence showing you understand their problem
  • Value prop: one sentence on what you do and one result you deliver
  • Social proof: one line — "We helped [similar company] achieve [result]"
  • CTA: one low-friction ask — "Would a 15-minute call this week work?"
  • Total length: under 100 words

Follow-up sequence — 4-email framework

  • Email 1 (Day 1): Cold email with offer and CTA
  • Email 2 (Day 4): Add value — share a relevant resource or insight
  • Email 3 (Day 10): Different angle — highlight a different pain point or benefit
  • Email 4 (Day 16): Breakup email — "Totally fine if now isn't the right time. Should I reach out again in Q2?"

Breakup emails often get the highest reply rate of the sequence — people respond to finality.

Handling objections with AI

Common objections: "We already have a solution", "Not in the budget", "Send me more information", "I need to talk to my team". For each objection, use the Objection Handling prompt — provide the objection, your product details, and ask for 3 response options (direct, empathetic, reframe). Pick the one that matches your style and edit it.

Does AI-written sales copy actually work?

AI-written copy works when it's personalised and edited. A generic ChatGPT cold email sent to 1000 prospects performs poorly. A ChatGPT-drafted email, personalised with specific details about each prospect, and edited to match your authentic voice, performs as well as a human-written one. Use AI for the framework and structure; add the personalisation yourself. The research, the trigger, and the voice must be human.
