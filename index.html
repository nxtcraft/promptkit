What makes a good AI prompt?

  • Specific goal — tell the AI exactly what output you want
  • Context — who you are, what the task is for, who the audience is
  • Format instructions — length, structure (bullet points, numbered list, paragraphs), tone
  • Constraints — what NOT to include, word limits, rules to follow

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between ChatGPT and Claude prompts?

Both ChatGPT (by OpenAI) and Claude (by Anthropic) are large language models that understand natural language instructions. The same prompt generally works well on both — the main differences are in response style. Claude tends to follow formatting instructions more precisely, while ChatGPT may be more creative. All prompts on PromptKit are tested to work on both.

How do I use a prompt from PromptKit?

Click "Copy Prompt" on any card. Open ChatGPT (chat.openai.com) or Claude (claude.ai). Paste the prompt. Replace the [placeholders] with your actual information. Press Enter. The AI will generate a tailored output in seconds.

Can I customise these prompts?

Yes — in fact, that's the point. Every prompt uses [brackets] to mark what you should customise. You can also add more context, change the format instructions, or combine parts of different prompts to create your own.

Do these prompts work with the free version of ChatGPT?

Most prompts work well with the free version of ChatGPT (GPT-3.5). For complex tasks like code review, SWOT analysis, or long-form writing, GPT-4 or Claude 3 will give significantly better results.
