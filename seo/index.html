ChatGPT for SEO — Content Strategy, Keywords & Optimisation

ChatGPT is a powerful SEO assistant for the tasks that don't require real-time data: content briefs, title tag variations, meta descriptions, internal link anchors, and topical cluster planning. For data-dependent tasks (search volumes, rankings, backlink counts), you still need Ahrefs, Semrush, or Google Search Console.

What ChatGPT can and cannot do for SEO

  • ✅ Generate keyword variations and search intent classifications
  • ✅ Write and optimise title tags and meta descriptions
  • ✅ Create detailed content briefs with outline and FAQ sections
  • ✅ Plan topical clusters and internal linking strategies
  • ✅ Rewrite existing content to target a specific keyword naturally
  • ❌ Provide real search volume data (use Ahrefs/Semrush/GSC for this)
  • ❌ Check live rankings or competitor backlink profiles
  • ❌ Guarantee what Google will rank (no tool can do this)

Content brief workflow with ChatGPT

  1. Use the Content Brief prompt with your target keyword and intent
  2. Ask ChatGPT to list the top questions users have about this topic (for FAQ section)
  3. Ask it to identify 5 related keywords to use naturally in the article
  4. Ask for 3 internal linking opportunities from your other content
  5. Write the article yourself or use the Writing prompts to draft sections

Title tag optimisation tips

  • Keep under 60 characters to avoid truncation in SERPs
  • Put the primary keyword near the start of the title
  • Add a benefit or modifier: "Free", "Complete Guide", "2025", "Calculator"
  • Include brand name at end: "| SiteName"
  • Ask ChatGPT for 10 variations and A/B test in GSC
Should I use AI to write full SEO articles?

You can use AI to write first drafts, but publishing unedited AI content at scale is risky. Google's Helpful Content system and manual reviewers look for: original insights, expert perspective, specific facts and examples, and content that satisfies the search intent better than existing results. Edit every AI draft: add your own data, examples, and expert opinion. The goal is content that is better than the current top 3 results, not just different.
