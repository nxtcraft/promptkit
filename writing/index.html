ChatGPT Prompts for Writers and Bloggers

These writing prompts work with ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini. For long-form content, Claude tends to produce better-structured output with cleaner formatting. For creative writing, ChatGPT-4 often feels more natural.

How to use ChatGPT for blog posts without sounding robotic

  • Always ask for a specific tone: "write in a conversational, first-person tone"
  • Provide your own examples and ask ChatGPT to incorporate them
  • Use prompts one section at a time — don't ask for a full 2000-word post in one go
  • Edit the output and add your own stories and opinions before publishing
  • Ask ChatGPT to "avoid AI clichés like: delve into, it's important to note, in conclusion"

Best workflow for AI-assisted writing

1. Use the Blog Outline prompt to get the structure. 2. Write the introduction yourself or use the Intro Hook prompt. 3. For each H2 section, paste the heading and ask ChatGPT to expand it in 150 words. 4. Add your personal experience and examples. 5. Use WordKit to check word count and reading time.

How do I make AI writing sound more human?

Add specific numbers, personal anecdotes, and opinions that only you would have. Use short sentences. Start sentences with "And", "But", "So" occasionally. Vary sentence length. Ask ChatGPT to "vary sentence structure and avoid starting every sentence with 'The' or 'This'". Reading the text aloud is the quickest way to spot robotic sections.
