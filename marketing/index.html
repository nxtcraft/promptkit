ChatGPT Prompts for Marketing — Complete Guide

These marketing prompts work with ChatGPT (GPT-4), Claude, and Gemini. Each prompt is structured with clear instructions and [placeholders] — replace the brackets with your specific details.

How to write ChatGPT prompts for ads

For ad copy, always include: the product name, the target audience (age, location, interest), the key benefit (not feature), the desired tone, and the platform (Facebook, Google, LinkedIn). Ask for multiple variations so you can A/B test.

ChatGPT for email marketing

The best email prompts specify: who you're writing to, what action you want them to take, the subject line (ask for 3 options), and a word limit. For newsletters, include a "content pillar" — the theme of this issue — so the AI doesn't write something generic.

Tips for better marketing prompts

  • Give ChatGPT a persona: "Act as a direct response copywriter with 10 years of experience"
  • Always specify tone: conversational, authoritative, playful, urgent
  • Ask for multiple versions: "Write 3 variations of this headline"
  • Include real data when you have it: CTRs, conversion rates, customer testimonials
Can I use ChatGPT to create an entire marketing campaign?

Yes. Start with the Campaign Brief prompt to set the strategy, then use individual prompts for each deliverable: ad copy, email sequence, social media posts, and landing page copy. Feed the brief into each subsequent prompt so ChatGPT maintains consistency across the campaign.
